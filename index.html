About This Curriculum

This world history curriculum is organized into two sequential 180-lesson courses spanning the full scope of human history from prehistory to the present day. Each course follows the Easy Peasy All-in-One High School format, with Khan Academy providing the chronological spine, Easy Peasy supplying the lesson and assignment style, and the VMS research framework providing the note-taking, book-study, essay, and final-paper architecture.

The supplied Vedic, devotional, and creationist texts are treated as identifiable sources with their own frameworks — not silently merged into a single "neutral" narrative. Students learn to compare mainstream historical reconstruction, creationist origin models, and Vedic frameworks, identifying presuppositions and evaluating evidence standards.

How This Course Works

📖

Khan Academy Spine

Khan Academy World History provides the chronological narrative. Each lesson references the relevant Khan Academy unit.

✍️

Writing-Intensive

Daily written work replaces traditional chapter tests. Paragraphs, essays, source analyses, and research notes demonstrate mastery.

🔬

Source Comparison

Mainstream, Vedic, creationist, and devotional sources are labeled and compared — students learn to distinguish evidence from interpretation.

🗺️

Cumulative Timeline

Students build a master timeline throughout the course. The final exam is an oral presentation of this timeline.

📚

Long-Form Reading

Seven substantial books across multiple categories, with structured chapter notes and a final research paper.

🎯

Research Paper

A staged research process culminates in a footnoted paper with bibliography, following the VMS research framework.
